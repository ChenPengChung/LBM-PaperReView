## Key Equations/Ideas

### Comparison Framework
**Interpolation-Based WENO:**
$$u_i^{interp} = \sum_{k=0}^{r-1} \omega_k^{interp} u_k(x_i)$$

**Reconstruction-Based WENO:**
$$\bar{u}_i^{recon} = \frac{1}{\Delta x}\int_{x_{i-1/2}}^{x_{i+1/2}} u(x) dx = \sum_{k=0}^{r-1} \omega_k^{recon} \bar{u}_{i+k}$$

where $u_k(x_i)$ are point values from polynomial reconstructions.

### Numerical Dissipation
The effective viscosity or dissipation error can be quantified through:
- Spectral analysis of the discretization scheme
- Error norms at different wavenumbers
- Turbulent kinetic energy decay rates

### Taylor-Green Vortex Test
Initial conditions:
$$u(x,y,z,t=0) = u_0\sin(2\pi x/L)\sin(2\pi y/L)\sin(2\pi z/L)$$
$$v(x,y,z,t=0) = -u_0\sin(2\pi x/L)\sin(2\pi y/L)\sin(2\pi z/L)$$
$$w(x,y,z,t=0) = 0$$

Performance metrics:
- Kinetic energy decay
- Enstrophy evolution
- Spectral energy distribution
- Shock preservation (if applicable)

## Critical Insights
1. **Interpolation-based schemes introduce measurable additional dissipation** compared to reconstruction-based schemes
2. **Dissipation patterns vary with flow scales** and depend on the effective resolution
3. **Taylor-Green vortex provides quantifiable benchmark** for comparing WENO variants
4. **The choice of interpolation vs. reconstruction has non-negligible impact** on turbulence simulation accuracy
5. **Compressible flow context matters:** results from incompressible methods may not directly transfer

## Experimental Setup (TGV)
- Domain: Periodic box $[0, 2\pi]^3$
- Initial Mach number: Typically $M_a \approx 0.1$ to 1.0
- Resolution: Multiple grid resolutions tested
- Time integration: High-order Runge-Kutta (typically RK3 or RK4)
- Final time: Long enough to observe significant energy decay
