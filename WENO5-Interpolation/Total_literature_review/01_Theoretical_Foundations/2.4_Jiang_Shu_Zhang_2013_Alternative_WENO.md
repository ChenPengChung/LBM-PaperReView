## Summary (中文)

本论文提出了基于WENO插值(而非重建)的有限差分格式的另一种表述。与传统方法不同,该论文直接使用WENO插值从点值重建任意位置处的函数值,然后计算通量。关键创新在于明确展示了线性权重$d_r$如何随插值目标位置变化而调整。结合Lax-Wendroff时间离散化,该方法提供了高阶保守格式。这种替代表述对于在GILBM框架中实现通量计算特别有启发。

## Key Equations/Ideas

- **Alternative FD Formulation Based on WENO Interpolation**:
  $$u_i^{n+1} = u_i^n - \frac{\Delta t}{\Delta x}(f_{i+1/2} - f_{i-1/2})$$
  where flux values are computed via WENO interpolation at cell interfaces

- **WENO Interpolation for Flux Calculation**:
  $$f_{i+1/2} = f(\hat{u}_{i+1/2}), \quad \hat{u}_{i+1/2} = \sum_{r=0}^{k-1} w_r(1/2) p_r(1/2)$$

- **Adaptive Linear Weights**:
  $$d_r(\sigma) = \text{Lagrange basis linear coefficients at position } \sigma$$

- **Lax-Wendroff Time Discretization Integration**:
  $$u_i^{n+1} = u_i^n - \frac{\Delta t}{2\Delta x}(f(u_{i+1/2}^{n+1}) - f(u_{i-1/2}^{n+1})) + O(\Delta t^2, \Delta x^{2k})$$

## Notes

This paper offers practical guidance for implementing WENO interpolation in finite difference schemes, particularly valuable for the GILBM_WENO5 project as it shows exactly how to structure a scheme based on WENO interpolation at arbitrary points. The explicit treatment of position-dependent linear weights and the Lax-Wendroff framework directly support our implementation needs.
