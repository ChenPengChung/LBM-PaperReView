## Key Equations/Ideas

- **WENO Interpolation from Point Values**:
  $$u(x) = \sum_{j=i-k+1}^{i} u_j \ell_j(x)$$
  where $\ell_j(x)$ are WENO-weighted Lagrange basis functions

- **WENO Interpolation Weights at Arbitrary Point**:
  $$\ell_j(x) = w_j(x) \tilde{\ell}_j(x), \quad \tilde{\ell}_j(x) = \prod_{l \neq j} \frac{x - x_l}{x_j - x_l}$$

- **Interface Interpolation at Subdomain Boundary**:
  $$\hat{u}(\text{interface}) = \sum_{r \in \text{left}} w_r^L p_r^L + \sum_{r \in \text{right}} w_r^R p_r^R$$
  with proper weighting for matching from both sides

- **Flux Continuity Condition**:
  $$f(\hat{u}_{\text{interface}}^-) = f(\hat{u}_{\text{interface}}^+) \text{ at interfaces}$$

## Notes

This paper is particularly valuable for GILBM_WENO5 because it demonstrates the complete process of constructing WENO interpolation from point values and applying it at subdomain interfaces. The framework for handling arbitrary interface locations and ensuring proper flux evaluation directly supports the GILBM scheme's multi-block or multidomain structure. The point-value-based approach aligns naturally with Gauss-Legendre-Boltzmann methods that work with point-wise solution values rather than cell averages.
