## Key Concepts

- **Negative Linear Weights Problem**: When interpolation target is not at half-grid points, linear weights can become negative
- **Position-Dependent Weights**: Comprehensive analysis of how linear weights $d_r(\sigma)$ vary with target location $\sigma$
- **Positive-Splitting Framework**: Solution method to decompose weights into non-negative components
- **Arbitrary Target Points**: Critical for WENO interpolation at locations other than $x_{i+1/2}$
- **Physical Realizability**: Ensuring WENO approximations maintain correct sign for physical quantities

## Summary (中文)

本论文直接解决了WENO插值在非半网格点处面临的关键问题:线性权重可能为负。论文详细分析了当插值目标不是标准的$x_{i+1/2}$而是任意点σ时,线性权重如何随位置变化且可能为负。最重要的是,论文提出了正分解(positive-splitting)框架,该方法将负权重分解为非负权重的组合。这项工作对于在GILBM方案中使用任意点σ的WENO插值至关重要。

## Key Equations/Ideas

- **Linear Weights at Arbitrary Point**:
  $$d_r(\sigma) = \prod_{l=0, l \neq r}^{k-1} \frac{\sigma - l}{r - l}$$
  can be negative when $\sigma$ is not at half-grid points

- **Positive-Splitting Framework**:
  $$d_r(\sigma) = d_r^+(\sigma) - d_r^-(\sigma)$$
  where $d_r^+(\sigma) \geq 0$ and $d_r^-(\sigma) \geq 0$

- **Decomposed WENO Weight**:
  $$w_r(\sigma) = \frac{d_r^+(\sigma) w_r^+ - d_r^-(\sigma) w_r^-}{\sum_s (d_s^+(\sigma) w_s^+ + d_s^-(\sigma) w_s^-)}$$

- **Weight Non-Negativity**: Analysis of conditions on smoothness indicators to ensure $w_r(\sigma) \geq 0$
