## Key Equations/Ideas

- **WENO Interpolation at Arbitrary Point**:
  $$\hat{u}(\sigma) = \sum_{r=0}^{k-1} w_r(\sigma) p_r(\sigma)$$
  where weights depend on position $\sigma \in [0,1]$

- **Linear Weights Adaptation**:
  $$d_r = d_r(\sigma) \text{ varies with target location}$$

- **Smoothness Indicator-Based Weights**:
  $$w_r(\sigma) = \frac{\tilde{w}_r(\sigma)}{\sum_s \tilde{w}_s(\sigma)}, \quad \tilde{w}_r = \frac{d_r(\sigma)}{(\epsilon + IS_r)^p}$$

- **Positivity Preservation**: Theory for ensuring $w_r(\sigma) \geq 0$ for all $\sigma$
